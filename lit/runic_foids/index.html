In the deep mountain ranges on the planet ballastria in the pingus 19 constellation live the hilltop peoples the shiv nors. each shiv nor tribe has a scholar trained with pretext of thousands of years of ancient scribal knowledge. Their physical language consists of delicate knot structures that are woven into fabrics made from their butt hairs. the shiv nors only grow hair out of their butts, which they call "the area of revealing." Their butt knowledge is quite obtuse, stemming from a culture of deep reverence and purpose. From the time a shivnor priest is born he is assigned the role of memorizing thousands of yards of knots interwoven in to their giant library tapestry. Warring tribes fight over this butthair knowledge taking whatever scraps of tapestries they can get their hands on, and burning the darker texts. Before the shamans go through their rites of initiation they must first eat nothing but the rotten berries of the ankleberry shrubs for seven days and seven nights. and on the seventh night the biotoxins within the berries become fully released, and they are met with the great visage of the ancestral spirit. somtimes this spirit can take the form of an ancestor. somtimes the spirit is nothing but a reverse chair. but the deepest of shamens often report seeing a grand fractal being known as the shamenga ham hemoleth. often called the great ham hemoleth, it is a very strange and intricate interdimensional being made out of shifting interwoven butthairs shifting in and out of dark portals and passages. This being will invite the shaman into one of these murky passages. In real life their body begins to seize as they have the vision of the beyond lands. In these lands infinite stretches of jungle pass through ancient ruins of the ancient peoples of the snatch, whos memories exist only in etchings in their elaborate stonemasonry. Shamans who linger in these ruins might never return to their bodies, and will eventually die in real life. but the ones who come back tell tales of sentient bird people and speak a new language called bird light language. only seventeen shamans in the last ten thousand years have ever come back from the other side with knowledge of this avian light language. and each of them possessed some great ability to shift reality by voicing words of power.
