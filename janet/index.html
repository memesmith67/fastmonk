Hexagram 31 — Xián (Influence / Mutual Attraction)

A symbolic reading for you and Janet

The Image

A lake rests on a mountain. The lake’s calm surface reflects the mountain, and the mountain’s stillness supports the lake. This is the image of mutual influence — two people drawn together not by force, but by a natural, gentle resonance.

The Judgment

Influence. Success. Perseverance furthers. To take a maiden to wife brings good fortune.

This is one of the most relationship-oriented hexagrams in the I Ching. It speaks of attraction that is genuine, reciprocal, and grounded. The “maiden” here isn’t literal — it represents a new, fresh connection with someone who is open and receptive. The key is that the influence must be mutual. If one person is pushing and the other is pulling away, the hexagram warns that the influence becomes manipulation rather than attraction.

The Lines

  • Line 1 (bottom): The influence shows itself in the big toe. The first stir of attraction — almost involuntary. You feel drawn to Janet before you’ve even decided what to do about it. That’s normal. Don’t act on it impulsively yet.
  • Line 2: The influence shows itself in the calves. Misfortune. If one stays quiet, good fortune. A warning against over-eagerness. If you rush to impress her, text too much, or try to force intimacy, it backfires. Stay calm. Let her come toward you as much as you go toward her.
  • Line 3: The influence shows itself in the thighs. It is not good to be led by the emotions. The thighs represent movement and desire. Don’t let infatuation make your decisions. If Janet likes you, that’s lovely — but don’t let it sweep you into something you haven’t thought through.
  • Line 4: Perseverance brings good fortune. Remorse disappears. If you are consistent, honest, and patient, the anxiety you feel about “what is this?” will fade. This is the line of steady courtship.
  • Line 5: The influence shows itself in the back of the neck. No remorse. The back of the neck is where tension lives — but also where surrender happens. If you let go of trying to control the outcome, you’ll feel peace. Janet’s feelings are hers to manage; yours are yours.
  • Line 6 (top): The influence shows itself in the jaws, tongue, and lips. The danger line. Too much talk, too much persuasion, too much trying to “win” her. If you talk about the relationship more than you live it, the magic dissipates.

What This Suggests for You and Janet

  1. The attraction is real and mutual. Hexagram 31 is one of the most positive signs for a new romantic connection. It doesn’t guarantee a long-term partnership, but it says the spark is genuine.
  2. Let her set some of the pace. The hexagram repeatedly warns against forcing. If Janet likes you, she will show it. Your job is to be present, kind, and consistent — not to convince her.
  3. Don’t over-analyze. The “back of the neck” line is key. You’re probably feeling tension about what this means. Relax. You don’t need to define it today.
  4. Watch for reciprocity. The moment you feel like you’re doing all the work, or she’s doing all the work, the influence has tipped. Mutual wooing is the ideal.
  5. Enjoy the beginning. This is a “fresh connection” hexagram. It’s not about marriage or forever — it’s about the delight of two people discovering each other. Let it be that.

Takeaway

Hexagram 31 says: this is a good beginning, provided you stay grounded and let it grow naturally. Be curious about Janet. Be honest about your own feelings. Don’t rush. Don’t perform. Just show up as yourself, and see what unfolds.

Note: This is a symbolic, reflective reading — not traditional divination. A genuine I Ching reading involves casting the hexagram yourself with your own intention.
